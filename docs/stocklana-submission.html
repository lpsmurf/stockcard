Project name

StockCard

Tagline / one-liner (280 max)

Spend what you own — never sell it. A self-custodial credit card backed by tokenized stocks (xStocks, SPCX, pre-IPO). Lock collateral in an Anchor vault, borrow USDC, spend it with a card. Cashback buys more of your stock.

Short description

StockCard is a self-custodial credit line for stock holders. Lock tokenized US stocks (xStocks, Backpack's SPCX) in an on-chain vault, borrow USDC up to a conservative LTV at a fixed APR, and spend it with a virtual card. Card cashback automatically buys more of your stock and adds it to your collateral. Repay anytime, withdraw your stock — you never trigger a sale.

Long description (5000 max — 3,824 chars)

StockCard is a credit card backed by your stocks — Nexo, but for equities, self-custodial, on Solana. THE PROBLEM Stock holders who need cash face a bad trade-off: sell (lose the position, trigger taxes, miss upside) or wait days for a broker margin loan. DeFi lending exists, but it's built for crypto natives, not stock holders. WHAT IT DOES - Lock tokenized stocks in an on-chain vault (Anchor program, devnet) - Borrow USDC instantly up to a max LTV at a fixed APR — credit-line style, borrow and repay freely - Spend through a virtual card (issuer-agnostic CardProvider interface; a real devnet USDC transfer settles each purchase to a merchant address) - Card cashback buys more of your chosen stock and deposits it into your collateral — cashback is an asset, not points - Send to bank: borrow USDC and route it to a SEPA payout (simulated rail on devnet) - Position protection: warning bands at 55/60/65% LTV, alert banners and web push with exact add-collateral or repay amounts, plus a "crash price" admin demo (−30%) that shows a live liquidation end-to-end - Savers: a USDC pool funds the loans (target ~6% APY to savers, spread to the protocol) WHY SOLANA The collateral, the loan and the card settlement are all real transactions on one network. Tokenized equities already trade on Solana (xStocks are Token-2022 with the Scaled UI Amount extension) — StockCard is the consumer credit layer that makes holding them better than a brokerage account. Instruction-level LTV enforcement in under a second is only practical on Solana. HOW IT'S BUILT - Anchor 1.2 program: Config / Market / Position / SignedPrice PDAs. Every borrow, withdraw and liquidate re-checks LTV against a fresh signed price inside the same instruction — collateral safety lives in the program, not the client. Checked integer math only, u128 intermediates, interest rounded in the protocol's favor. - Token-2022 collateral: xStocks-style mints with the Scaled UI Amount multiplier; positions value raw amount × multiplier × price × (1 − haircut), matching how real xStocks behave (issuer controls, permanent delegate, freeze authority). - Price signer: a server-side oracle posts a signed price only when at least two of {issuer API (xStocks/Backpack), Jupiter, Pyth Hermes} agree within 200 bps. A third source drops a single outlier instead of going dark. Equity prices deliberately go stale when the market closes — the program refuses unsafe borrows after-hours. - App: one Next.js 16 PWA is both the website and the Android app (solana-mobile webshell + Mobile Wallet Adapter). Calm consumer-fintech UI, not a DeFi dashboard. PRE-IPO COLLATERAL (Tessera + PreStocks bounties) StockCard accepts pre-IPO SPV tokens as collateral — live on devnet today: OpenAI and Kalshi (Tessera T-tokens), Anthropic and SpaceX (PreStocks). The price signer reads each provider's public API and lends against the LOWER of NAV and traded price, so a token trading at a premium never inflates the credit line. On-chain they are Equity markets with a tighter band (30% max LTV, 25% haircut, 11.9–15.9% APR). Buy them in the Demo Shop, deposit, borrow — end-to-end on devnet. WHAT'S REAL VS MOCK (honest demo) - Real: every collateral deposit, borrow, repay, liquidate, savings and cashback transaction; the devnet USDC transfers behind card settlement and payouts; the signed-price oracle reading three real data sources. - Mocked and labelled in the UI: the card issuer (mock provider; Bridge sandbox integration built but gated), the SEPA rail (mock payout address), and the devnet stock/USDC mints (real xStocks have no devnet mints). No real KYC. Try it: https://stockcard-app.vercel.app — connect a devnet wallet, claim test dUSDC in the Shop, buy NVDAx, deposit, borrow, spend on the card. Pyth bounty paragraph (Best use of Pyth market data)

Pyth is wired into StockCard's collateral price signer (app/src/lib/prices/pyth.ts, Hermes client). Today Pyth supplies the NYSE market-hours calendar that gates every equity price post — prices deliberately go stale when the market closes, which drives the LTV checks on the Anchor program. With a Pro grant, Pyth equity feeds become a third price source: it breaks ties between the issuer API and Jupiter, lets the signer drop a single outlier instead of going dark, and covers an issuer API outage — so a lending protocol can keep pricing collateral safely when one feed fails. Pyth never prices collateral alone by design.

Tessera + PreStocks bounty notes

Four live pre-IPO collateral markets on devnet: T-OPENAI and T-KALSHI read Tessera's public token-details API (markPrice), PRE-ANTHROPIC and PRE-SPACEX read the PreStocks public API (markPrice + tokenPrice). The signer posts min(NAV, traded price) so a premium never inflates collateral value. Demo: buy pre-IPO tokens in the Demo Shop → deposit → borrow dUSDC against 30% LTV. Code: app/src/lib/prices/preipo.ts, market params in app/src/lib/config.ts (preIpo band: 30% LTV / 25% haircut / 11.9–15.9% APR).
